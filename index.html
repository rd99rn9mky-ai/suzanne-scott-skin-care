About

I have practiced as an esthetician for over 30 years. Over that time, my work has grown into a holistic approach to skincare.

My treatments are restorative. They combine classic European techniques with intuitive, holistic rituals. Every facial is custom. I use connective tissue release and manual lymphatic drainage to help the skin lift and sculpt. The same work soothes the nervous system and restores balance.

I see clients in two places. In San Francisco, I work at Yelena’s Spa on Geary Street on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays. In Marin, I see clients at my studio in San Rafael on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Fridays.
